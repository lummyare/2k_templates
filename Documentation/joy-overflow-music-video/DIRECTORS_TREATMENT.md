**Director's read:** the song never drops into a sad section. It is joy from the first bar. So the **pain lives only in the pictures**, and the music is the future already speaking to the past. ## 3. THE THREE WORLDS

### A-ROLL: "THE WAREHOUSE" (performance)
- **Location:** a large disused warehouse, e.g. in the Apapa/Ikeja industrial area. Concrete floor, steel trusses, high windows blacked out, heavy haze.
- **Key image:** the artist in the **W6 hero costume**, alone in a **single hard top spotlight** (4 ft circle), a **vintage chrome microphone stand** with a classic dynamic mic in front of him. He grips the stand with both hands, sways, lifts it, and tilts it back at the climaxes.
- **The crew is part of the picture:** camera operators in **all-white crew uniforms** (white overalls/caps) running **white-bodied cinema rigs** such as RED V-Raptor and ARRI Alexa 35 in white cages, a **Technocrane**, a **dolly on tracks**, a **Steadicam op**, and an FPV drone pilot. They are lit as silhouettes and rim-lit shapes around the spotlight. The audience should *feel* a major production is happening around this man.

  > If you meant something different by "white camera men", tell me and I'll adjust. The idea as written is to show the scale of the production.

- **Light build across the song:** Intro has one spotlight only. Chorus 1 adds backlight haze beams. Verse 2 brings in practical tungsten bulbs across the trusses. Bridge flashes strobe white on "Chains are broken". Final chorus lights the whole warehouse in gold, and the crew start to dance. Outro ends with gold confetti falling through the spotlight.

### B-ROLL 1: "GRASS" (micro-drama, Lagos lows)
Ojuelegba / Oshodi under-bridge, Balogun Market, Marina/Broad Street office towers, a Surulere music studio, and Third Mainland Bridge at night in the rain.

### B-ROLL 2: "GRACE" (micro-drama, Lagos highs)
Private jet terminal at Murtala Muhammed Airport, Eko Atlantic glass towers, Lekki–Ikoyi Link Bridge, an Ikoyi boardroom, a company HQ with his name on the building, and a return to the same bridge where he slept.

## 6. LIP-SYNC & PERFORMANCE DIRECTION

### 6.1 On set (live shoot)
1. **Playback at 100% speed** for all 24/25 fps takes. For slow-motion lip-sync (48/60 fps), **play the track back at 2× / 2.4× speed** so the mouth matches at normal speed in the edit.
2. Every A-roll setup gets **3 full-song passes** (wide / medium / close) so the editor can choose freely.
3. Slate with **timecode sync**: a Tentacle/Deity TC box on the playback laptop and every camera.
4. The artist must **sing out loud and full voice**, not mime. Real breath, neck tension and jaw movement are what make it look real.

### 6.2 Expression map (natural, not over-acted)
| Section | Face | Body / Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| Intro / Verse 1 | Eyes closed → open, soft remembering, slight frown of memory | Still, both hands on the stand |
| Pre-chorus | Brows lift, chin rises | One hand leaves the stand, opens outward |
| Chorus | Full smile, teeth visible on "hallelujah", eyes sparkle | Rock the stand on beats 2 & 4, shoulder bounce, hand raised on "shout" |
| Verse 2 | Confident, warm, story-telling | Hand to chest on "overflow my heart" |
| Chant break | Playful, grinning, looking at the crew | Point the mic to the crew for call & response, footwork |
| Bridge | Fierce, eyes intense on "chains are broken", then release | Stand slam on the beat, fist clench → open palm |
| Final chorus | Tears plus smile, head back | Arms wide, spin, dance away from the stand |
| Outro | Peace, gratitude | Hand on heart, head bow |

### 6.3 If using AI video / lip-sync (Kling, Veo, Hedra, HeyGen, Runway Act-Two, etc.)
- Generate the **still key frame first** (Section 5), approve the identity, then animate. **Never generate the face from text alone.**
- Feed the lip-sync engine **audio clips cut exactly to the shot timecodes** in Section 4. Use 3–8 s per clip, trimmed on phrase boundaries.
- Use the **isolated vocal stem** for lip-sync if the producer can export it. The full mix with drums causes mouth "chatter" on the snare.
- Plosives to check frame by frame: **"P"raise, "B"lessings, "M"ountains, "M"ercy**. The lips must fully close. On **"Hallelujah"** the tongue lifts on "L", and **"Joy"** needs rounded lips.
- Add in every animation prompt: *"natural singing expression, realistic jaw and throat movement, breathing between phrases, no exaggerated mouth, identity unchanged from reference."*
- Run a **face-consistency pass** on every final clip against REF-4. If the likeness drifts, regenerate. Do not "fix" it in post.

## 8. COLOUR & EDIT RHYTHM

- **Act 1 (Grass):** teal-grey, low saturation, crushed blacks, 35mm grain, sodium orange nights. Cuts roughly every **2 bars (5.4 s)**, so the pace feels slow and heavy.
- **Act 2 (Turn):** colour gradually returns. Cuts every **1 bar (2.7 s)**.
- **Act 3 (Grace):** warm gold and rich navy, clean highlights. Cuts every **2 beats (1.35 s)** in the dance breaks and every **half bar** in the THEN/NOW montage.
- **Warehouse:** a fixed look with neutral-cool blacks and a warm skin key, so the artist's skin tone is **always** accurate and consistent.
- **Transitions:** match cuts (face to face, gesture to gesture), light-flash cuts on "Hey!" and "Chains are broken", and no gimmicky wipes.
